# Digitization of Speech Signals: A Practical Application of Spectrogram Analysis

**Author:** Jusuf Dacic
**Course:** Digital Signal Processing, Faculty of Traffic and Communications, University of Sarajevo
**Date:** June 2025
**Grade:** 10/10
**Tools:** MATLAB

## Description

This project applies the principles of analog-to-digital signal conversion and spectrogram analysis to real speech recordings. Speech was recorded in three modalities - normal tone, whispered, and raised pitch - using the same spoken sentence, then digitized and analyzed in MATLAB.

The work covers the full signal chain: sampling and quantization of the analog voice signal, short-time Fourier transform (STFT) for time-frequency representation, spectrogram generation and interpretation, low-pass Butterworth filtering to remove high-frequency noise, and signal reconstruction using interpolation.

Each speech modality was compared across its time-domain waveform and frequency-domain spectrogram, both before and after filtering, to observe how vocal effort and articulation affect the acoustic signal. The analysis also includes a comparison of common speech analysis tools (Praat, MATLAB, CSL, TF32, WaveSurfer) and a discussion of real-world applications of digital speech processing, including voice assistants, telecommunication codecs, and speaker authentication systems.
