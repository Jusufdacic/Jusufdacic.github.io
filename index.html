Research

Robustness and Limitations of ML/DL ADS-B Spoofing Detectors: A Review of Open Problems

2026 · In Progress
Aviation Security  ·  ADS-B  ·  Machine Learning  ·  Literature Review

A review of machine learning and deep learning approaches to detecting spoofed ADS-B signals in air traffic communication. The paper examines how robust these detectors remain against evasive attacks, and identifies the problems that stay unresolved across the current literature. Being prepared for ATCT, the International Conference on Advances in Traffic and Communication Technologies, organised annually by the Faculty of Traffic and Communications, University of Sarajevo.

Digitization of Speech Signals: A Practical Application of Spectrogram Analysis

2025 · Undergraduate Academic Project
Digital Signal Processing  ·  MATLAB  ·  Grade 10/10

Analog-to-digital conversion and short-time Fourier analysis applied to real speech recordings, comparing normal, whispered and raised-pitch speech across the time and frequency domains, with Butterworth filtering and signal reconstruction.
